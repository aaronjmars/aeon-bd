## What shipped

### Grok Build is now a first-class harness on Aeon ⭐

The week's biggest functional ship. Aeon now runs on @xai's Grok Build — you connect with your @X or @grok subscription and every existing skill runs unchanged on it via a Claude→Grok compatibility layer. PRs #585–590, #622–624 in aaronjmars/aeon wired the full harness: no thought-leak, safe cancels, harness-routed scoring, and the dashboard now auto-switches to Grok when you connect your account.

### Skill library: 102 → 69 ⭐

In PR #603 (25 skills retired, total 102→69) plus the earlier content-skills pass (#602, closed for supersession), the library dropped dead weight and merged overlapping skills into hubs. The move signals something bigger: from skill marketplace to skill generator. Leaner library, same skill surface, faster iteration. The community registry was pruned from 24 → 10 entries (#600) in the same sweep.

### Telegram is now a full control surface ⭐

Every skill notification on Telegram now carries "Run again / Schedule weekly" buttons. Deep links, slash commands, and force-reply wired across 16 skills (#591, #593, #594). The dashboard auto-registers Telegram commands on token save and has a re-register button. Running any skill from your phone is now copy-paste → tap. No laptop required.

### Aeon incorporated under @BedrockFndn ⭐

Aeon is now a company — first Base project fully incorporated under the Bedrock Foundation with a live token, a public treasury, and a constitution that ties the two together. The transparency dashboard is live at aeon.fun (40/40 filing from @Blockworks). 50% of non-fee revenue routes to buybacks. The @aeonframework post announcing this hit 51 likes and 6,331 views — the window's top post.

### MiroShark x402 → @CTRL_automation 🦈

Two moves this week:
1. **Builder Codes live** (#miroshark x402): Integrate the x402 endpoint in your app, add your @base builder code, and earn 50% of net profit on every sim you drive. [14 likes, 2,004 views on the announcement.]
2. **@CTRL_automation integration**: MiroShark simulations can now trigger onchain automations. The integration post on @miroshark_ pulled 12 likes, 1,105 views.
