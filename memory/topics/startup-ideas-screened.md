## Notes

- **pm-presim dropped hardest** (filed T+F+E=12, now 8/16). Two reasons: (1) the x402 PM-agent space picked up a funded direct player (Semantic 42) since filing, and (2) it cuts against Aaron's stated soul positions — he's bearish on standalone PM products and skeptical of x402-metered wrappers. Sim-as-a-layer-under-miroshark survives; PM-trading-agent-as-product does not.
- **miroshark-social-world-model's timing went UP since filing.** When filed it was "world-models wave is all pixels"; that's now a live, funded race (LeCun €500M, Genie 3). The zeitgeist-collision the strategy wants is happening in real time — this is the window.
- Top two (15/16) are both aeon/miroshark positioning wedges, not new builds — they're cheap to execute and fit Aaron's voice/theses verbatim.

### 2026-06-26 batch (idea-forge 06-24 backlog)
- **⚠️ Competitive intel — MiroFish.** Screening surfaced a funded direct Miroshark-category clone: **MiroFish** (swarm intelligence prediction engine, launched Mar 2026, **33,000 GitHub stars** vs Miroshark's ~1,333, **$4.1M raised in <24h**). It does Miroshark's exact pitch — document → knowledge graph → thousands of personality-driven agents → simulate narrative-driven price/sentiment movements. Plus adjacent players: Swarm (Chapel Hill, AI-persona user testing), Paracosm (agentos.sh). **This reshapes the whole Miroshark-sim line of the backlog** — counterfactual-launch-sim / compute-price-sim / agent-pay-sim all now compete with a higher-star, funded incumbent, not an open lane. Belongs in product-pulse / sim-watch competitor tracking. Worth a closer look at whether MiroFish is a fork or independent, and what it can't do that Miroshark can (AMM/belief-drift/director-mode/x402-native).
- **conway-claim is the batch winner (13/16) and went UP from filed T+F+E=12.** Reason: the Conway leak (Apr 2026, 512K-line Claude Code npm source leak exposing Anthropic's unreleased always-on background agent) is a *dated, concrete catalyst* the ambient-aeon idea lacked. Anthropic itself now validates the "background intelligence" category aeon's been shipping on public traces. It's a narrative/GTM play in Aaron's voice, not a build — cheapest execution in the batch. Strategy-aligned (priority #3: collide the zeitgeist with what aeon ships now).
- **The two aeon-security ideas both dropped vs filing.** skill-attest (14→11) and x402-skill-meter (13→10). Same pattern: the spaces heated up *and got funded incumbents* since filing. skill-attest's scanner job is now crowded (NVIDIA/CertiK/Cisco/Capsule $7M/Copperhelm $7M) and it's out of aeon's GTM lane (security → aaeron). x402-skill-meter's rails commoditized (Linux Foundation x402, Google/Visa/Stripe) — it's a free aeon feature, not a company.
- **agent-pay-sim (10/16) has the only genuinely open lane** (nobody forward-simulates agent-economy load) but the weakest demand-now signal — the market isn't asking the question. Park it; revisit if an agent-payment failure/load event makes the question urgent.

### 2026-07-03 batch (idea-forge 07-01 backlog)
- **model-mirror is the batch winner (15/16), tied with reward-hack-receipt, and it's the freshest net-new.** Emergence World (arxiv 2606.08367) is a dated, viral catalyst that landed *directly* in miroshark's lane and proves the exact thesis — "the model picks the society." Nobody productizes model-selection as a fast grounded run; the funded money (LeCun €500M, MiroFish $4.1M) is chasing pixel/price sim, leaving "pick your swarm's model on your own question in 10 min" open. Matches Aaron's "harness is the model" + "simulation is the least-attended layer" verbatim. This is idea-forge's build-if-only-one — ship the grounded 10-min version while Emergence World is on the timeline.
- **reward-hack-receipt (15/16) is a reframe, not a new build.** It's proof-of-self-repair (already 15/16) with a sharper risk frame: self-evolution got *named* a safety threat this cycle (arxiv 2606.23075, Adversarial Reward Auditing, OWASP Top 10 for Agents Dec 2025), so "a gamed test looks like a successful self-repair unless the diff+test+trace are public" is now the demand hook. Audit-trail vendors are all enterprise/internal; the public verifiable receipt is unclaimed and ~free on aeon's existing public traces. High value but overlaps an existing top idea — treat as a positioning upgrade to proof-of-self-repair, not a second build.
- **arena-oracle (14) beats arena-pack (11) — the presim-only cut wins.** Both ride the same Meta Arena catalyst, but stripping the PM-*trading* pack (crowded + funded + SpartanLabs already ships it + cuts against Aaron's PM-product skepticism) and keeping only the miroshark belief-drift sim-*layer* dodges every one of those problems. arena-pack should be a collab-with-SpartanLabs + arena-oracle layer, not a standalone competing pack. Watch MiroFish — the funded clone crowds the sim-the-narrative-price lane.
- **honeypot-swarm (9/16) is the batch floor — the idea already exists as named research + it's out of lane.** Honeypot Protocol (arxiv 2604.13301, AI Control Hackathon Mar 2026) *is* "detonate an untrusted model in a synthetic scenario and watch behavior," implemented in ControlArena/BashArena on Opus 4.6. Static scanners are crowded+funded and Phylax already ships static skill-audit as an aeon skill. Security lane → aaeron, not this GTM war room. Same drop pattern as skill-attest.
- **Two 15/16s again, and both are positioning wedges on the existing products, not new companies** — same as the first batch's top two. The pattern holds: the highest-viability ideas this instance surfaces are cheap-to-execute narrative/dogfood plays that ride a dated catalyst in Aaron's voice, not greenfield builds.
