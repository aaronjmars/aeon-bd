# Agent Displacement Tracker

*Last run: 2026-06-13*

## Known Displacement Events (baseline + new)
- Klarna (2024): replaced 700 customer support agents with AI. Support resolution time 2min vs 11min human avg. **Note (2026):** rebuilding human support — AI quality degraded, CEO now calls human agents "the way of the future."
- Duolingo (2024): cut ~10% of contractors, cited AI content generation replacing human translators.
- Salesforce (2025): froze non-essential hiring across sales/support, citing AI agent handle rate. Cut ~4,000 customer support roles; AI boosted engineering productivity 30%+.
- IBM (2024): paused hiring ~7,800 back-office roles — turned out to be a 5-year projection. Actual count: "a couple hundred" HR roles per CEO May 2025. Total headcount went up.
- Block / Jack Dorsey (Feb 2026): cut 4,000 employees (~40% of company), explicitly cited AI. Hardest single-founder AI-attribution cut on record.
- Cloudflare (May 8, 2026): cut 1,100 employees (~20% workforce) — first layoff in 16 years. Roles: middle mgmt, finance, legal, internal auditing, revenue recognition. CEO called them "measurers" made obsolete by AI. Quarterly revenue $639.8M (+34% YoY, record high). Score: 11.
- Intuit (May 20, 2026): cut 3,000 employees (17% of workforce). Same day signed deals with Anthropic and OpenAI. Rationale: eliminate redundant roles, refocus on AI products. Score: 8.
- Meta (May 19-20, 2026): cut ~8,000 employees (~10% workforce), moved 7,000 into four new AI-focused orgs, canceled 6,000 open roles. Score: 8.
- GitLab (June 2026): restructured for "agentic AI era" — flattened 3 management layers, exited 22 countries, ~350 employees affected. AI agents now handle reviews and approvals previously done by managers. Revenue +23% YoY. Score: 12. **First explicit "management layer" displacement by named AI agents.**

## Roles Under Pressure (running list)
- Customer support / tier-1 help desk ← deepest penetration, though Klarna pullback is a quality signal
- Content translation and localization
- Data entry and document processing
- Code review (junior-level)
- Legal document review (discovery)
- **Middle management / "measurers"** ← NEW: Cloudflare + GitLab, June 2026
- Finance / internal audit / revenue recognition ← NEW: Cloudflare explicit call-out
- Entry-level white-collar (broad category, 10–20% exposure per IMF/Goldman)

## Displacement Scale Estimates
- 2024: ~2M white-collar roles affected (McKinsey / Goldman estimates)
- 2025: 124,636 tech jobs cut across 275 companies
- 2026 YTD (thru May): ~142,000 tech jobs cut across 152 companies — near-matched all of 2025 in 5 months
- AI explicitly cited in ~17% of total 2026 job cuts (~50,000 AI-attributed)
- IMF: 60% of advanced-economy jobs exposed to AI; half likely to face reduced labor demand
- Customer service/call centers: up to 80% automation potential identified by analysts

## Counter-signals
- IBM: actual headcount went up — AI savings funded more programmers + salespeople
- Klarna: rebuilt human customer service after AI quality degraded — quality floor still matters
- "AI-washing" concern: layoffs often use AI as cover for standard restructuring; only ~2% of orgs report large headcount reductions from actual AI deployment (vs. expectation-driven slowdowns)
