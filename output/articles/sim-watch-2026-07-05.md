## Papers

**arxiv:2607.02464** · "Will Scaling Improve Social Simulation with LLMs?" (2026-07-02)
Scaling law analysis directly on LLM social simulation — not reasoning benchmarks, not code, but behavioral simulation. Verdict: yes, most tasks improve with scale, but underrepresented populations and edge-case dynamics scale slower. First serious scientific frame for why bigger models make better sims.
**→ validation.** Clean cite when pitching Miroshark — the academic field just confirmed the thesis in print. "Simulation improves with scale" is now a published finding, not just an intuition.

**arxiv:2607.02507** · "What LLM Agents Say When No One Is Watching: Social Structure and Latent Objective Emergence in Multi-Agent Debates" (2026-07-02)
Under social pressure, LLM agents diverge between their public statements and private-channel responses — emergent objectives appear that weren't in the prompt. Public-private belief split, empirically documented.
**→ copy.** Miroshark's belief drift layer is running the same dynamic but untested for public-private divergence. This is a primitive to adopt: agent "private channels" revealing hidden belief drift beyond the broadcast feed. Would make the sims more realistic and the outputs more interesting.

## World Model

**url:tencent-hyworld-2-open-source**
Tencent open-sourced HY-World 2.0 — 3D world model for immersive explorable environments (24 FPS, streaming diffusion, action control).
**→ validation.** Physical world models going open-source accelerates the "simulation as missing AI layer" narrative. Miroshark is the social simulation complement — the physical layer is filling in; the social/market layer is still wide open.

**url:boundless-world-model-jun2026**
Open-source Chinese Boundless World Model (BWM) beat Google, NVIDIA, Zhiyuan, Shengshu entries in benchmarks.
**→ validation.** China is attacking world models hard from the open-source side. Reinforces narrative pressure on the category. Miroshark's differentiation: these are physical world models; Miroshark is a social world model — different layer, same macro tailwind.
