## Competitors

**`uno-km/AMEVA-Dead-Internet-Threatre`** · 1 star · Jun 21 · verdict: **counter**
Solo Korean researcher, fully autonomous Dockerized sim of "Dead Internet Theory." Agents run as separate Docker containers (Llama 3.1 8B), a Director LLM injects perturbations and prevents deadlock, and a LPDE personality tensor (2D Affect, 4D Opinion, 2D Power) drives belief drift across rounds. Sequential, local-only, no AMM, no platform integration. Conceptually the same loop as Miroshark. Miroshark's edge: scale (hundreds of agents, not sequential containers), actual X/Reddit integration, AMM-coupled belief drift, x402-native, counterfactual branching. The LPDE personality tensor is worth copying—adding affect/opinion/power state vectors to Miroshark agents would make belief drift more grounded than current scalar drift.

**`eq-network/Collective-Intelligence-Library`** · 6 stars · Jun 28 · verdict: **opening**
Uppsala University (Jonas Hallgren). JAX-native functional MAS framework — pure functions over a typed GraphState, category-theory composition of transforms, `lax.scan`-style execution, `jax.vmap` for 1000-seed adversarial sweeps on a single GPU. Flagship experiment: basin stability under adversarial pressure on resource allocation games. Research code, no social layer, no AMM. The opening: they're doing what Miroshark's counterfactual branching does (parallel fork-the-timeline runs) but in JAX with a PhD thesis as the interface. Miroshark's angle: "counterfactual branching without the tensor algebra."

## World Model

**Genie 3 (Google DeepMind) + GWM-1 (Runway)** · Jun 2026 · verdict: **validation**
Two new physical world models landed this week—Genie 3 generating interactive photorealistic environments, GWM-1 for real-time simulation from prompts. Physical world models are now a consensus VC bet ($1B+ in World Labs, NVIDIA Cosmos 3, now Genie 3 / GWM-1). The emerging framing: Fei-Fei Li's taxonomy (Renderer / Simulator / Planner) is hardening into industry canon. Physical sim is locking up. Social/discrete-opinion simulation is next and still open. Timing: the "simulation is the missing layer" narrative is now being said by Yann LeCun and DeepMind, not just Fei-Fei Li. Miroshark should ride this hard in positioning.

**`arxiv:2606.27326`** · "Hallucination in World Models is Predictable and Preventable" · Jun 25 · verdict: **opening**
Hallucination in physical world models concentrates in low-coverage state-action regions. The paper's fix: detect coverage gaps before rendering. Miroshark's angle: discrete social/opinion simulation has no such problem—belief state is finite and enumerable, not a continuous physics manifold. Use this as a contrast point when pitching: "physical world models hallucinate at the edges; social sims let you branch those edges instead."
