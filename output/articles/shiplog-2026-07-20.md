## MiroShark model training

The biggest signal move of the week — not a commit, a deliberate quality investment.

Aaron fine-tuned **Qwen3.5-9B** (via Tinker) specifically for MiroShark simulation agent quality:
- Corpus: ~9k curated originals from ~11 respected accounts filtered to ~4,041 tweet→create_post action pairs
- Architecture: LoRA (rank 32) SFT on Qwen3.5-9B instruct, `qwen3_5_disable_thinking` renderer
- Goal: given a persona + feed, emit a native create_post tool-call that reads like a real human — trained on real tweets reframed as actions, not generic "write a tweet" instructions
- Result: **~50% quality improvement** on SFT model alone. DPO pass next.

Thread (07-17): **23♥ / 5RT** — [x.com/aaronjmars/status/2078213422626283961](https://x.com/aaronjmars/status/2078213422626283961)

The signal: not just feature-shipping, going deeper on quality at the model layer. The slop problem in agent-generated simulation posts is being attacked at the source.
