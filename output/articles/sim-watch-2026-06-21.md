# sim-watch — 2026-06-21

**Top move:** Doxa is the closest Miroshark competitor to date — same market+social simulation lane, actively shipping. Counter it now before it gets any stars.

---

## Competitors

### `VincenzoManto/Doxa` — counter
YAML-driven multi-agent sim for economic and social systems. LLM-backed agents, market microstructure, relation graphs, world events, FastAPI + React client. 13 stars, updated 2026-06-17.

The overlap with Miroshark is real: social agents + market mechanics + world events + YAML config. The gap: no real social platform data (X/Reddit), no AMM belief drift, no director mode, no counterfactual branching, no x402, no sub-$1 price point. Doxa is a lab toy; Miroshark is a production engine. But that gap narrows if Doxa gets a community. **Counter move: ship a Miroshark vs Doxa side-by-side on the same scenario, call it out explicitly.** Let the $1 / <10min number do the work.

### `francemazzi/worldsim` — competitor (low threat)
LangGraph-based stateless world sim engine, Node.js/TypeScript. 10 stars. More game-engine orientation than social sim. No market mechanics. **Monitor, not urgent.**

## Papers

### arxiv:2606.18795 — validation
*Opinion Polarization in LLM-Based Social Networks: Manipulation and Mitigation* (2026-06-17)
LLM agents in simulated social networks show belief states drifting under adversarial conditions despite mitigation attempts. Direct empirical validation of the Miroshark belief-drift thesis. **Cite this in the next Miroshark pitch and Show HN.**

### arxiv:2606.17657 — copy
*Using Cognitive Models to Improve Language Model Simulation of Human Persuasion Games* (2026-06-16)
Prompting LLMs to internalize cognitive models of human belief updating meaningfully improves persuasion simulation fidelity. Miroshark's agent prompting could adopt this — a cognitive-model layer on top of each agent's stance would make belief drift more realistic. **File as a sim primitive to add.**

### arxiv:2606.20493 — validation
*Contagion Networks: Evaluator Bias Propagation in Multi-Agent LLM Systems* (2026-06-18)
Systematic biases spread and amplify across interacting LLM agents. Validates the multi-agent interaction architecture; also a useful caution when designing director-mode interventions.

### arxiv:2601.21473 — copy
*ScaleSim: Serving Large-Scale Multi-Agent Simulation with Invocation Distance-Based Memory Management*
At hundreds of agents, memory pressure is the infra bottleneck. ScaleSim's distance-based eviction reduces aggregate memory demand significantly. **Miroshark should adopt this pattern when scaling past ~200 agents.**

### arxiv:2606.19144 — validation
*Human-AI Coevolution Dynamics: Formal Theory of Social Intelligence Emergence Through Long-Term Interaction* (2026-06-17)
Theoretical framework for multi-timescale social cognition dynamics. Background signal that the research community is formalizing what Miroshark is building empirically.

## World Models

### World Labs / Marble — validation
Fei-Fei Li's taxonomy (Renderer / Simulator / Planner) is now the dominant framing in this space. World Labs' Marble product is targeting the Simulator role — persistent 3D worlds from multimodal prompts. Nvidia Cosmos 3 (open-weight, June 2026) is going after physical sim. Both are physical-world plays. **Nobody in the world-model discourse is touching social/economic simulation** — that's still Miroshark's open field. The Li taxonomy is a gift: use it in pitches. Miroshark = the Simulator for social/market systems, the layer the physical-sim race ignores.
