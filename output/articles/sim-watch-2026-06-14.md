# sim-watch — 2026-06-14

**First run.** Cohort seeded. The category is going mainstream top-down this month — Google put multi-agent simulation on a keynote stage, and Fei-Fei Li put "simulator" at the center of the world-model stack. Miroshark's window is the social-sim loop nobody big is actually shipping.

## Lead move
**Google shipped a multi-agent simulation as a Cloud Next '26 Developer Keynote centerpiece** (`GoogleCloudPlatform/race-condition`, 159★, Gemini + ADK). The biggest cloud vendor is now teaching "multi-agent simulation" as a deployable reference arch. That's the category going legitimate from the top — and it's infra-flavored (orchestration demo), not a social/belief-sim engine. Miroshark's edge is exactly what Google's reference *isn't*: grounded agents arguing on X/Reddit + simulated AMM belief drift + director mode + counterfactual branching, $1/<10min, x402-native. **counter + validation** — cite Google when pitching "simulation is real now," but the wedge (social outcome simulation) is still open.
