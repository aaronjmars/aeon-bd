## Lead: MiroFish lineage resurfaces (threat)

**[servetkosem/BTC-SOL-ETH-S-COINS-NAME-N-B-NANCE-MARKET](https://github.com/servetkosem/BTC-SOL-ETH-S-COINS-NAME-N-B-NANCE-MARKET)** · 0★ · pushed 2026-07-07

"Offline multi-agent simulation & prediction engine. English fork of MiroFish with Neo4j + Ollama local stack."

Same ancestor as Miroshark. Different bet: goes local-first (Ollama), no AMM, no X/Reddit social layer, no x402, no cloud scale. Someone found the MiroFish lineage worth rebuilding — that's the thesis validated in the wild.

**Verdict: threat** — direct reimplementation of Miroshark's MiroFish ancestor. 0 stars today; watch for community traction.

Miroshark's edge: cloud-native, real X/Reddit personas, AMM belief drift, director mode, counterfactual branching, x402-native, $1/<10min at scale. Local Ollama can't social-simulate or price-compete.
